Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Famca, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and profile photo
  • Content: Photos and videos you upload to the app, and the captions and comments you write
  • Usage Data: Device information (OS, app version), usage statistics
  • Push Notification Token: If you enable notifications, we store your device token to deliver them

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Famca service
  • Authenticate your account and verify your identity
  • Enable sharing of photos and videos within your family groups
  • Send you push notifications about new posts and activity (if enabled)
  • Improve our app and user experience
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our service

3. Data Sharing

We share your data with the following third-party service providers necessary to operate the app:

  • Group Members: Photos, videos, captions, comments, name, and profile photo are visible to members of your family groups
  • Google: Sign-in is handled through Google OAuth. Google receives your sign-in request and returns your email, name, and profile photo (google.com)
  • Expo: Push notifications are delivered through Expo’s push service, which receives your device token and the notification text (expo.dev)
  • Supabase: Database, authentication, and backend infrastructure (supabase.com)
  • Cloudflare R2: Media file storage — photos and videos are stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (cloudflare.com)
  • GIPHY: If you use the GIF feature, search queries are sent to GIPHY's API (giphy.com). GIPHY's privacy policy applies to this data
  • We NEVER sell your data to third parties or advertisers

4. Data Security

We take security seriously and protect your data using:

  • Industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Presigned URLs for secure, time-limited access to media files
  • Row-level security policies ensuring only group members can access shared content
  • Regular security audits and updates

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR and Slovak law, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Download: Export your data directly from Settings → Download My Data
  • Correct: Update or correct your name and profile photo in Settings
  • Delete: Permanently delete your account and all associated data via Settings → Delete Account
  • Object: Object to certain processing of your data by contacting us

You can exercise these rights in the app's Settings page or by contacting us at privacy@famca.app.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • Photos and videos: Automatically and permanently deleted 24 hours after they are posted. You can also delete a post yourself at any time before then
  • Captions and comments: Deleted together with the post they belong to
  • Account information: Kept until you permanently delete your account
  • Usage data: Retained for up to 90 days for analytics purposes
  • Push notification tokens: Retained while your account is active; deleted when you delete your account

7. Children's Privacy

Famca is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us immediately at privacy@famca.app.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored on servers located in the European Union or other regions (including Cloudflare's global infrastructure). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for any international transfers in accordance with GDPR requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes via email or in-app notification. Your continued use of Famca after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data:

  • Email: privacy@famca.app
  • Support: support@famca.app
